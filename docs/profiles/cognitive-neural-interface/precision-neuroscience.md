# Precision Neuroscience

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Precision Neuroscience Corporation
**HQ Location:** New York, NY, USA (additional sites in Santa Clara, CA and Addison, TX)
**Founded:** 2021, by Benjamin Rapoport, Michael Mager, Demetrios Papageorgiou, and Mark Hettick ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Precision_Neuroscience))
**Flagship product:** **Layer 7 Cortical Interface**
**Website:** https://precisionneuro.io

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Cognitive Disorders / Neural Interface
**Primary Indication:** Brain-computer interface applications spanning epilepsy/tumor mapping and (longer-term) communication/motor restoration for paralysis ([FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/fda-clears-precision-neurosciences-minimally-invasive-brain-computer-interface-implant)).

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Cerebral cortex surface (subdural, non-penetrating)
**Delivery Mechanism:** The **Layer 7 Cortical Interface** is an ultra-thin (thinner than a human hair) micro-electrode array of **1,024 electrodes** that lies directly on the cortical surface without penetrating brain tissue, sliding beneath the skull through a small slit ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/04/17/brain-implant-cleared-fda-musk-neuralink-rival-precision-neuroscience.html); [GlobeNewswire clearance release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/04/17/3063418/0/en/Precision-Neuroscience-Receives-FDA-Clearance-for-High-Resolution-Cortical-Electrode-Array.html)). It can both record and stimulate electrical activity on the brain's surface.
**Biological basis:** This is an **electrocorticography (ECoG)**-based interface — a well-established, decades-old clinical recording modality (used routinely for epilepsy surgical mapping) now being adapted for chronic BCI use. See the peer-reviewed review of ECoG-based BCI state of the art ([Miller, Hermes & Staff, *Neurosurgical Focus* 2020 — PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32610290/)) and the original Seattle ECoG-BCI experience paper ([Leuthardt et al., IEEE TNSRE 2006 — PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16792292/)).

## Regulatory Status & Clinical Trial

- **FDA 510(k) clearance** granted **April 17, 2025** for the Layer 7 array, authorizing implantation in patients for **up to 30 days** for clinical/mapping use ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/04/17/brain-implant-cleared-fda-musk-neuralink-rival-precision-neuroscience.html); [MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/precision-neuroscience-brain-implant-fda-cleared/745913/)).
- This is reported as the **first full FDA regulatory clearance granted to a company developing a next-generation wireless BCI** ([MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/precision-neuroscience-brain-implant-fda-cleared/745913/)).
- The clearance covers short-duration (≤30-day) clinical use (e.g., intraoperative/epilepsy mapping), **not** a chronic/permanent implant indication — **[unverified]** whether a chronic-use submission has since been filed; not found in this research pass.

---

## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. Blackrock Neurotech / Paradromics (intracortical/penetrating arrays):** Layer 7 does not penetrate brain tissue, which may reduce chronic biocompatibility/gliosis risk at the cost of lower single-neuron spatial resolution than penetrating electrodes.
- **vs. Synchron (endovascular):** Precision's array sits directly on the cortical surface (higher signal fidelity than vascular recording) but requires a craniotomy/cranial access procedure, unlike Synchron's catheter-based approach.
- First BCI company in this next-generation wireless cohort to reach a full FDA clearance (rather than only Breakthrough Device Designation or IDE status), a meaningfully different regulatory milestone than most peers in this tracker.

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- Current clearance is time-limited (≤30 days), not yet a chronic/permanent implant approval — commercial chronic-use deployment timeline **[unverified]**.
- ECoG surface recording generally offers lower spatial/single-unit resolution than penetrating intracortical arrays (a general field-level tradeoff, not specific to Precision) — see review literature above.
- Highly competitive, well-capitalized field (Neuralink, Synchron, Paradromics, Blackrock); relative long-term clinical performance data across companies is not yet available industry-wide.
- Exact addressable market size for next-generation BCI was not independently sized in this research pass — **[unverified]**; broader neuromodulation device market estimated at $6.37B (2025) growing to $13.93B by 2033 is a poor proxy since it includes unrelated device categories ([ResearchAndMarkets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/5644957/neuromodulation-devices-market-size-share-and)).
