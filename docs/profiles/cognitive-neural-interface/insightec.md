# Insightec Ltd. (Exablate Neuro)

**Category:** Cognitive Disorders / Neural Interface (essential tremor, Parkinson's tremor; investigational Alzheimer's/psychiatric) | **Status:** Private | **HQ:** Tirat Carmel, Israel | **Founded:** 1999

## Funding & Company Status
Total disclosed funding ≈$574M cumulative. Most recent: $150M equity financing closed June 2024, led by Fidelity Management & Research Company, co-led by Nexus NeuroTech Ventures and Ally Bridge Group, with Baillie Gifford, Catalio Capital Management, Fayez Sarofim & Co., Gilmartin Capital and existing investors. Reportedly closed at a $375M valuation, down from a prior ~$1B valuation. A separate 2024 announcement referenced financing "up to $200 million." Key historical investors: GE Healthcare, Elbit Imaging, York Capital Management.

## Target Nerve / Mechanism
Non-invasive, incisionless thermal ablation of the ventral intermediate nucleus (VIM) of the thalamus (unilateral or staged bilateral thalamotomy) for essential tremor and PD tremor; pallidothalamic tractotomy/pallidotomy targeting the globus pallidus for advanced Parkinson's. Delivered via >1,000 individually phased ultrasound transducer elements in a stereotactic helmet, focused transcranially under real-time MRI guidance, ablating a sub-millimeter focal point with no incision or implant. Separately, lower-intensity focused ultrasound is used investigationally to transiently open the blood-brain barrier (BBB) to enhance antibody delivery in Alzheimer's trials.

## Biological Basis
The VIM thalamus is a key relay node in the cerebello-thalamo-cortical (CTC) tremor-generating network; disrupting pathological oscillatory synchrony there interrupts tremor circuitry. Foundational evidence: Elias et al. (pilot, *NEJM* 2013, doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1300962) and pivotal randomized sham-controlled trial (Elias et al., *NEJM* 2016, doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1600159). Recent work (Frontiers in Neurology, 2025) shows successful thalamotomy is associated with normalized CTC resting-state functional connectivity. For BBB-opening, NEJM 2024 (doi:10.1056/NEJMoa2308719) shows transient tight-junction disruption increases local antibody penetration.

## Market Size / Affected Population
Essential tremor: global pooled prevalence ~1.33% (PMC8035980); ~24.9M affected globally (2020). US: ~1.1M adults with diagnosed ET (2024, PMC12551629), rising to 1.61% in ages ≥75. Parkinson's disease projected to affect >1.2M Americans by 2030. Focused ultrasound systems market (all indications) estimated ~$1.8B (2026) growing to ~$6.14B by 2035. Insightec-specific neuro-indication revenue not independently disclosed.

## Technology
(1) Patient fitted into a stereotactic frame with a water-cooled helmet of >1,000 phase-controlled ultrasound transducers; (2) MRI/CT fusion confirms target; (3) ultrasound beams focused/phase-corrected to converge with sub-millimeter precision; (4) low-energy "test" sonications progressively raise tissue temperature while patient is awake and neurologically tested in real time; (5) therapeutic-energy sonications create a permanent thermal ablation lesion under MR thermometry feedback; (6) no incision, no implant, typically same-day discharge.

## Novelty / Differentiation
Unlike DBS (Medtronic, Boston Scientific, Abbott), which requires craniotomy and an implanted electrode/pulse generator, Exablate is entirely incisionless with no permanent implant — trading reversibility/adjustability (DBS) for a one-time ablative solution. Versus Gamma Knife radiosurgery, provides real-time intra-procedural clinical/MRI feedback. Its BBB-opening application is a differentiated drug-delivery-enabling modality unlike anything else in this category.

## Limitations — Practical
Requires favorable skull density ratio (SDR) — some patients ineligible; needs an MRI suite plus specialized stereotactic equipment, limiting site availability; procedure can take 2–4+ hours with head immobilized; ablation is thermal and permanent, so targeting errors can cause irreversible side effects (sensory disturbance, gait/balance problems in the pivotal trial); currently FDA-approved mainly for unilateral treatment per session, bilateral requires a staged second procedure ≥9 months later.

## Limitations — Theoretical / Readiness
Unlike DBS, thermal ablation is not reversible or adjustable after the fact. Long-term (many-years) durability of tremor benefit and delayed-onset side effects still being characterized. The Alzheimer's BBB-opening application remains investigational — a device-enabled drug-delivery adjunct, not yet FDA-approved, with ultimate clinical benefit beyond safety/feasibility not established. Ethical/patient-selection concerns exist around irreversible brain ablation for functional conditions; access constrained by limited specialized centers.

## Regulatory & Clinical Status
FDA PMA approval July 2016 (P150039) for unilateral thalamotomy in medication-refractory essential tremor. Expanded 2018 to tremor-dominant Parkinson's disease. Later approved for staged bilateral/second-side ET treatment. FDA approved staged bilateral pallidothalamic tractotomy for advanced Parkinson's disease (2025/2026). Investigational: BBB-opening trials for Alzheimer's ongoing (West Virginia University Rockefeller Neuroscience Institute since 2018, Weill Cornell Medicine), interim 10-patient data published in Journal of Neurosurgery showing successful, reversible BBB opening with no adverse events.
