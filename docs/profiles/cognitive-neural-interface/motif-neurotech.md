## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Epidural cortical surface (dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, optimal placement over gyral crowns)
**Delivery Mechanism:** The **DOT microstimulator** is a millimeter-scale (~9mm wide), wireless, battery-free implant placed in the epidural space (above the dura, not penetrating brain tissue) that delivers bursts of electrical stimulation through the dura to the cortical surface, powered wirelessly via magnetoelectric power transfer developed at Rice University; designed for a ~20-minute outpatient implant procedure ([Rice News](https://news.rice.edu/news/2024/rice-team-demonstrates-miniature-brain-stimulator-humans); [Medical Design & Outsourcing](https://www.medicaldesignandoutsourcing.com/motif-neurotech-dot-brain-pacemaker-neurostimulator-design-depression/)).
**Biological basis:** Epidural cortical stimulation (ECS) is a peer-reviewed neuromodulation approach; the underlying device physics and cortical field modeling are described in **"Miniature battery-free epidural cortical stimulators"** ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38608028/)) and a follow-on placement-optimization study ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42413552/)), both associated with the Rice research group underlying Motif's technology.

## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. Neuronetics/BrainsWay (TMS):** Motif's DOT is a fully implanted, always-available device (no daily in-clinic visits required after implantation) vs. TMS's requirement for repeated clinic sessions over weeks.
- **vs. SetPoint Medical/traditional VNS-style implants:** DOT is dramatically smaller (millimeter-scale) and battery-free, implanted via a short outpatient procedure rather than a more invasive surgical implant with a battery/generator.
- Novel wireless magnetoelectric power-transfer approach, distinct from RF or inductive coupling used by most existing implanted neurostimulators.

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- Still in **early feasibility/first-in-human** stage; no confirmed efficacy data from a controlled trial was found in this research pass — **[unverified]**.
- Epidural (non-penetrating) stimulation of the cortex is a less spatially precise targeting approach than intracortical or even subdural (ECoG-surface) stimulation — general field-level tradeoff.
- Reliance on a novel wireless magnetoelectric power-transfer mechanism introduces engineering risk not yet proven at commercial scale.
- Company funding history beyond the January 2024 Series A is sparsely documented — **[unverified]**.
