# Paradromics

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Paradromics, Inc.
**HQ Location:** Austin, Texas, USA
**Founded:** 2015 ([Austin Startups](https://austinstartups.com/companies/paradromics-inc); [PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/paradromics-raises-33-million-in-funding-achieves-breakthrough-medical-device-designation-from-fda-301827969.html))

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Cognitive Disorders / Neural Interface
**Primary Indication:** Severe motor neuron disease / ALS and paralysis — restoring communication (thought-driven text and synthesized speech) ([University of Michigan Health press release](https://www.michiganmedicine.org/news-release/university-michigan-implants-first-human-paradromics-wireless-brain-computer-interface-designed))

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Cortical motor/speech-related regions
**Delivery Mechanism:** A cortical array of **421 microelectrodes** connected to an **implantable chest transceiver**, decoding neuronal activity and broadcasting it wirelessly ([Neuroscience News](https://neurosciencenews.com/first-human-wireless-bci-implant-30908/); [Michigan Medicine](https://www.michiganmedicine.org/news-release/university-michigan-implants-first-human-paradromics-wireless-brain-computer-interface-designed)).
**Note on prior draft:** an earlier version of this profile described a "10,000+ channel" array — that figure is **not supported** by any source found in this research pass and appears to have been an unverified extrapolation. The confirmed, sourced figure is **421 electrodes**.

## Regulatory Status & Clinical Trial

- **FDA Breakthrough Device Designation**: granted, date not separately confirmed beyond the 2023 funding-round press release framing ([company press release](https://paradromics.com/news/paradromics-raises-33-million-in-funding-achieves-breakthrough-medical-device-designation-from-fda/)).
- **FDA Investigational Device Exemption (IDE)** granted for the **"Connect-One"** clinical study in **November 2025** ([MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/paradromics-fda-green-light-bci-study/); [company press release](https://paradromics.com/news/paradromics-receives-fda-approval-for-the-connect-one-clinical-study-with-the-connexus-brain-computer-interface/)).
- **Earlier safety signal:** in **June 2025**, Connexus was temporarily implanted in a patient during epilepsy-monitoring research, confirming the device could be safely placed and could record signals ([Neuroscience News](https://neurosciencenews.com/first-human-wireless-bci-implant-30908/)).
- **First permanent human implant** under Connect-One took place at **University of Michigan Health**, performed by Dr. Matthew Willsey and Dr. Aditya S. Pandey, in a female participant with severe motor neuron disease ([Michigan Medicine](https://www.michiganmedicine.org/news-release/university-michigan-implants-first-human-paradromics-wireless-brain-computer-interface-designed); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260617963732/en/Paradromics-and-University-of-Michigan-Complete-First-Connexus-BCI-Implantation-for-the-FDA-Approved-Connect-One-Clinical-Study); [MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/paradromics-first-implant-bci-feasibility-study/)).
- **Trial sites:** University of Michigan, UC Davis, and Massachusetts General Hospital are screening participants for Connect-One ([Neuroscience News](https://neurosciencenews.com/first-human-wireless-bci-implant-30908/)).
- **[unverified]** — no clinical efficacy/performance results from Connect-One were found in this research pass (the trial had only just begun implanting participants as of the sources found); do not cite performance figures for Connexus without checking for a later results announcement.

---

## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. Blackrock Neurotech (Utah Array):** Paradromics' Connexus is fully implantable with wireless transmission via a chest-mounted transceiver, vs. Blackrock's historically percutaneous/pedestal-based systems.
- **vs. Neuralink:** both are pursuing high-channel wireless implants; Paradromics reached first human implant under an FDA-approved IDE study (Connect-One) — a directly comparable regulatory milestone worth tracking against Neuralink's own trial status separately.
- Backed by Breakthrough Device Designation, which can expedite FDA review but does not itself constitute clearance or approval.

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- Still in **early feasibility / first-in-human** stage (Connect-One) — not commercially available, no confirmed timeline to market found in this research pass.
- **[unverified]** — cost, reimbursement pathway, and long-term (multi-year) signal stability data were not found in the sources reviewed.
- General field-level limitation shared with all intracortical BCIs: long-term biocompatibility and signal degradation of penetrating electrodes remains an open scientific question industry-wide.
