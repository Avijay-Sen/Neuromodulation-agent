## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Cognitive Disorders / Neural Interface (BCI-based vision restoration)
**Primary Indications:** Geographic atrophy due to age-related macular degeneration (AMD); also pursuing retinitis pigmentosa and Stargardt disease.

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Delivery Mechanism:** PRIMA is a wireless, photovoltaic subretinal implant (2×2mm, 30µm-thick crystalline silicon array with 378 photovoltaic pixels, each 100µm wide) implanted beneath the degenerated macula. AR glasses with a front-facing camera and infrared projector capture and process a scene, beaming it as pulsed near-infrared light onto the implant; the photovoltaic pixels convert light into electrical current that stimulates surviving inner retinal (bipolar) cells, restoring a form of central vision — no wires or external battery are needed for the implant itself ([NEJM abstract](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2501396)).
**Biological basis:** Palanker et al. (Science Corp/Stanford collaboration), "Subretinal Photovoltaic Implant to Restore Vision in Geographic Atrophy Due to AMD," *NEJM*, 2026 ([NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2501396); [company summary](https://science.xyz/news/new-england-journal-of-medicine-prima/)). Pivotal trial: **PRIMAvera**, registered as [NCT04676854](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04676854), an open-label, multicenter, prospective, single-group study in geographic atrophy due to AMD (n=38); 26 of 32 twelve-month completers showed ≥2 lines of visual-acuity improvement ([Ophthalmology Times](https://www.ophthalmologytimes.com/view/primavera-study-central-vision-improvement-with-subretinal-implant)).

## Novelty & Differentiation

Versus Second Sight's Argus II (epiretinal, 60 electrodes of 200µm, wired/transscleral power, indicated for retinitis pigmentosa), PRIMA is subretinal, with higher pixel density (378 pixels at 100µm) and wireless photovoltaic power via infrared (no transscleral wire), targeting geographic atrophy/dry AMD rather than RP. Pixium Vision, developer of an earlier-generation PRIMA-like device, discontinued operations; the exact mechanics of how Science Corp came to continue PRIMA's development (licensing vs. acquisition of related IP) could not be fully verified in this research pass — **[unverified]** deal specifics. Company marketing describes PRIMA as "the only treatment to restore functional central vision" in geographic atrophy — this superlative claim is company language and should be treated as marketing, not independently verified superiority.

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- Prosthetic visual acuity is fundamentally capped by pixel pitch — best reported acuity is approximately 20/438–20/460, tied to the 100µm pixel size (smaller pixels would theoretically be needed for sharper vision).
- Requires extensive vision rehabilitation/training to use effectively; visual field is narrowed when using zoom.
- Adverse events, mostly implantation/procedure-related, cluster in the first 2 months post-surgery, with 81% of serious AEs occurring in that window (95% resolved within 2 months) ([Ophthalmology Science](https://www.ophthalmologyscience.org/article/S2666-9145(24)00046-0/fulltext); [NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2501396)).
- Requires surgical implantation (subretinal surgery) with attendant surgical risk; not applicable to patients without sufficient inner-retina/bipolar-cell survival.
