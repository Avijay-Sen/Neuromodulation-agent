# Blackrock Neurotech

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Blackrock Neurotech (formerly Blackrock Microsystems)
**HQ Location:** Salt Lake City, Utah, USA
**Founded:** 2008, by Florian Solzbacher and Marcus Gerhardt, who acquired the research business of Cyberkinetics Inc. (CKI) that year ([Our Story, Blackrock Neurotech](https://blackrockneurotech.com/our-story/)).
**Ownership:** Majority-owned by Tether (the stablecoin issuer) since April 2024 — see Funding below. Not affiliated with the asset manager BlackRock, Inc.
**Website:** https://blackrockneurotech.com

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Cognitive Disorders / Neural Interface
**Primary Indication:** Paralysis/Spinal Cord Injury (communication and device control via brain-computer interface)
**Secondary Indications:** ALS, locked-in syndrome, brainstem stroke

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Primary motor cortex (M1) and other cortical areas involved in movement planning
**Delivery Mechanism:** Surgical implantation of the **Utah Array** — a 10×10 penetrating microelectrode grid invented by Richard A. Normann at the University of Utah — directly into cortex ([bionic-vision.org](https://www.bionic-vision.org/companies/blackrock-neurotech)).
**Recording Type:** Single-unit and multi-unit action potentials from individual neurons near each electrode tip
**Commercial/investigational system name:** **MoveAgain BCI System**

---

## Biological Basis

Voluntary movement is encoded in primary motor cortex via distributed neural population activity; individual neurons fire at rates related to intended movement direction/speed. Blackrock's electrodes record this activity to decode motor intent in real time.

**Key foundational reference (general neuroscience, not Blackrock-specific — verify independently before citing in a formal memo):**
Georgopoulos, A.P., Schwartz, A.B., & Kettner, R.E. (1986). "Neuronal population coding of movement direction." *Science*, 233(4771), 1416–1419.

## Regulatory Status

The **MoveAgain BCI System** received **FDA Breakthrough Device Designation in November 2021** ([company press release](https://blackrockneurotech.com/insights/blackrock-neurotechs-moveagain-brain-computer-interface-system-receives-breakthrough-device-designation-from-the-fda/); [MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/fda-grants-breakthrough-nod-to-blackrock-neurotechs-brain-computer-interface/); [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/blackrock-neurotech-nabs-fda-breakthrough-nod-for-system-uses-thoughts-to-control-devices); [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/blackrock-neurotech-s-moveagain-brain-computer-interface-system-receives-breakthrough-device-designation-from-the-fda)). Breakthrough Device Designation expedites FDA review but is **not** market clearance/approval — as of this writing the system has not been confirmed to have full FDA clearance/approval; **[unverified]** whether that has since occurred — check FDA.gov's device databases directly before relying on this.

**Clinical activity:** Blackrock's arrays have been used for two decades in research settings via the multi-site **BrainGate** consortium (Brown, Stanford, Mass General, and others). ## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. non-invasive BCIs (EEG):** far higher signal bandwidth and resolution, at the cost of requiring brain surgery.
- **vs. other intracortical BCI companies (Paradromics, Precision Neuroscience, Synchron):** Blackrock/the Utah Array has the longest clinical track record (two decades of BrainGate research use) of any player in this space; competitors argue higher channel counts or lower invasiveness as their differentiators.
- **Distinctive:** now backed by a well-capitalized (if unconventional) majority owner in Tether, which materially changes its funding runway versus typical venture-backed peers.

---

## Limitations: Practical

- Requires open cranial surgery (craniotomy) to implant; carries standard neurosurgical risks (infection, hemorrhage).
- Requires weeks of patient training/calibration to use effectively.
- **[unverified]** — cost of the device/procedure to a patient or health system was not confirmed via a credible source in this pass; do not cite a specific dollar figure without further verification.

## Limitations: Theoretical & Readiness

- Long-term (multi-year) signal stability of penetrating microelectrodes is a known open question industry-wide (glial scarring, electrode degradation) — this is a general field-level limitation, not specific to a single Blackrock trial result I can cite.
- No confirmed full FDA clearance/approval as of this writing (Breakthrough Device Designation only) — reimbursement and commercial pathway remain undetermined.
