# Rune Labs

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Rune Labs, Inc.
**HQ Location:** San Francisco, CA, USA
**Founded:** 2018 ([PitchBook company profile](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/265355-56); [Built In SF](https://www.builtinsf.com/company/rune-labs))
**Flagship product:** **StrivePD** (software care ecosystem, including Apple Watch-based symptom monitoring)
**Website:** https://runelabs.io

**Note on fit:** Rune Labs is a **software/data platform**, not a device that itself delivers energy to neural tissue. It is included here because it is directly integrated with implanted DBS therapy (via a Medtronic data-sharing partnership) and is a recognized player in the neuromodulation ecosystem, but it does not fit this tracker's mechanism-based technology tree the way stimulation-delivering companies do.

## Funding

Rune Labs raised a **$12 million strategic round** (announced January 2024) led by **Nexus NeuroTech Ventures**, with participation from existing investors Eclipse, DigiTx Partners, Moment Ventures, and TruVenturo GmbH, bringing total disclosed funding to **over $42 million** ([Healthcare IT Today](https://www.healthcareittoday.com/2024/02/06/rune-labs-unveils-12-million-strategic-round-to-accelerate-adoption-of-novel-parkinsons-technologies/); [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2024/01/11/parkinsons-symptoms-app-strive-pd-rune-labs/)).

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Cognitive Disorders / Neural Interface (movement-disorder digital biomarker & DBS-data platform)
**Primary Indication:** Parkinson's disease — symptom tracking (tremor, dyskinesia) and clinical decision support for patients on DBS therapy ([PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/rune-labs-secures-fda-clearance-for-parkinsons-disease-monitoring-through-strivepd-ecosystem-on-apple-watch-301566472.html)).

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** N/A — Rune Labs does not deliver stimulation itself.
**Delivery Mechanism:** StrivePD combines **Apple Watch accelerometer/gyroscope-based movement sensing** with a cloud software platform to continuously track FDA-cleared metrics (tremor, dyskinesia) alongside patient-reported outcomes, and — via a partnership with Medtronic — lets clinicians correlate this data with programming data from **Medtronic's Percept PC Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS)** device ([PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/rune-labs-secures-fda-clearance-for-parkinsons-disease-monitoring-through-strivepd-ecosystem-on-apple-watch-301566472.html)).
**Biological basis:** Wrist-worn accelerometry for quantifying parkinsonian tremor and dyskinesia is an established, peer-reviewed digital-biomarker approach; see the crowdsourced validation study of digital measures for Parkinson's severity prediction ([Parkinson's Disease Digital Biomarker DREAM Challenge — PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7979931/)).

## Novelty & Differentiation

- Unlike every other company in this tracker, Rune Labs is a **data/software layer**, not a device manufacturer — its moat is aggregating multimodal Parkinson's data (wearable + DBS device telemetry + genetics) rather than delivering novel stimulation.
- Direct commercial partnership with Medtronic (the dominant DBS incumbent) positions it as connective infrastructure across the DBS ecosystem rather than a competitor to DBS makers.

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- Business model depends on continued interoperability/data-sharing partnerships with device incumbents (principally Medtronic) — a platform risk not present for companies that sell a self-contained device.
- Consumer wearable-based sensing (Apple Watch) has known limitations in specificity/sensitivity for clinical-grade symptom quantification compared to purpose-built clinical sensors — a general field-level caveat.
