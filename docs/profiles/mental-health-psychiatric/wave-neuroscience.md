## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Mental Health / Psychiatric
**Primary Indication:** Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD); also marketed off-label historically for other conditions (see Limitations).

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Delivery Mechanism:** MeRT uses quantitative EEG (qEEG) to identify each patient's individual dominant/peak alpha frequency, then delivers repetitive TMS pulses at that personalized frequency — rather than a fixed ~10Hz/1Hz standard protocol — targeting prefrontal cortical sites ([Wave Neuroscience](https://www.waveneuro.com/mert)).
**Biological basis:** "EEG Guided Personalized rTMS (PrTMS) for Post Traumatic Stress Disorder," *Brain Stimulation* ([full text](https://www.brainstimjrnl.com/article/S1935-861X(21)00429-0/fulltext)) reported a mean 23-point PCL-5 reduction. Also: "Preliminary Observations of Personalized Repetitive Magnetic Stimulation (PrTMS) Guided by EEG Spectra for Concussion," *Brain Sciences*, 2023 ([PMC10452199](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10452199/)). Related rationale: "Therapeutic Effects of Individualized Alpha Frequency TMS on Negative Symptoms of Schizophrenia" ([PMC2632240](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2632240/)).

## Regulatory Status & Clinical Trial

- **FDA Breakthrough Device Designation** for MeRT as an adjunctive PTSD treatment, announced **Dec 3, 2024** ([KXAN/Cision](https://www.kxan.com/business/press-releases/cision/20241203LA70114/wave-neuroscience-receives-fda-breakthrough-device-designation-for-pioneering-ptsd-treatment-mert/); corroborated by [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/wave-neuroscience-breakthrough-device-designation-received-for-ptsd-treatment-mert/)) — this designation itself is not clearance, only an accelerated-review pathway.
- **Pivotal trial:** [NCT02990793](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02990793) — prospective, double-blind, randomized, sham-controlled, ~158 patients across 8 sites; started ~April 2022, primary completion ~Sept 2025 — dates should be independently re-confirmed against the primary ClinicalTrials.gov record. Conducted with Texas A&M Health / Institute of Biosciences and Technology ([BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/texas-a-and-amp-m-health-wave-neuroscience-announce-second-phase-of-ptsd-clinical-trial)).
- A second related registered trial, [NCT06081309](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/09/NCT06081309/Prot_SAP_000.pdf) ("EEG Personalized Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (eTMS) for PTSD"), also appears in the ClinicalTrials.gov index — whether this is the same Wave-sponsored trial or a separate academic one could not be confirmed — **[unverified]**.
- **FDA clearance announced June 11, 2026** ([Morningstar/PR Newswire](https://www.morningstar.com/news/pr-newswire/20260611la81286/wave-neuroscience-announces-fda-clearance-of-mert-for-ptsd)). Reported topline results: active arm improved 27 points on PCL-5 (versus a VA clinically-meaningful-change threshold of 8.5–12.5) and 68.2% reached remission at follow-up. Described by the company as "the first personalized, biomarker-guided neuromodulation technology to receive FDA clearance for PTSD." A third-party database references "K260402" as the 510(k) number for the MeRT System ([510kdatabase.net](https://www.510kdatabase.net/k260402/)), but this could not be cross-checked directly against FDA's own database in this research pass — **[unverified]**.

## Novelty & Differentiation

Conventional TMS devices (Neuronetics NeuroStar, BrainsWay Deep TMS) use a fixed stimulation frequency/target (typically left DLPFC) regardless of the individual patient; MeRT instead individualizes the stimulation frequency via qEEG. No prior FDA-cleared device had a PTSD indication, and — per Wave's own clearance announcement — no prior FDA-cleared PTSD device was backed by a double-blind, sham-controlled RCT.

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- qEEG-guided coil-frequency selection is still derived from scalp measurements, not direct brain imaging — it does not solve spatial-targeting precision.
- The evidence base beyond the pivotal trial is limited; many clinic marketing claims for MeRT (e.g., for autism, speech/eye-contact improvement) are **not** supported by peer-reviewed evidence.
- Historically (pre-2026-clearance), MeRT was an off-label, cash-pay use of an FDA-cleared TMS device rather than itself being FDA-cleared — important context because most "MeRT" marketing online predates and is distinct from the 2026 PTSD clearance.
- Funding history could not be reconciled across sources — **[unverified]**.
