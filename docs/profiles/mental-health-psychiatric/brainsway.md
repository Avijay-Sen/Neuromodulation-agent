# BrainsWay Ltd.

**Category:** Mental Health / Psychiatric (MDD, OCD, smoking cessation) | **Ticker:** NASDAQ: BWAY | **HQ:** Burlington, Massachusetts, USA (US commercial HQ) & Jerusalem, Israel (R&D) | **Founded:** 2003

## Funding & Company Status
Listed on Tel Aviv Stock Exchange before Nasdaq IPO April 16, 2019 at $11.00/ADS, raising ~$27.5 million gross. FY2024 revenue: $41.0 million (+29% YoY), gross margin 75%. FY2025 guidance $49–51M; actual FY2025 revenue reported ~$52.2M (+27.3%). Market cap ~$230M (Aug 2025) rising to ~$611M (June 2026) — approximate, per data-vendor figures.

## Target Nerve / Mechanism
Patented H-coil ("Deep TMS") housed in a helmet apparatus; different geometries target different circuits: **H1 coil** — bilateral, broader/deeper prefrontal cortex for depression; **H7 coil** — medial prefrontal cortex + anterior cingulate cortex for OCD; a bilateral lateral prefrontal cortex + insula coil for smoking cessation. The larger induced electric field reaches deeper/broader cortical volume than a standard figure-8 coil, at some cost of spatial focality.

## Biological Basis
Depression: Levkovitz et al., 2015, *World Psychiatry* 14:64–73 — first double-blind multicenter RCT of Deep TMS in MDD. OCD: Carmi et al., 2019, *American Journal of Psychiatry* 176(11):931–938 — 99 treatment-resistant OCD patients, 29 sessions over 6 weeks; active dTMS produced a mean 6.0-point Y-BOCS reduction vs. 3.3 for sham, 38.1% response vs. 11.1% sham, implicating hyperactive mPFC/ACC circuitry. Smoking cessation: Zangen et al., 2021, *World Psychiatry* — 262 chronic smokers, bilateral insula/lateral-PFC stimulation, 25.3% quit rate (active) vs. 6.4% (sham) at week 6, consistent with the insula's role in craving/interoception.

## Market Size / Affected Population
Same depression epidemiology as Neuronetics (WHO ~280M globally; ~3M US treatment-resistant adults). A precise dollar TAM for OCD/smoking-cessation Deep TMS specifically could not be verified; broader smoking-cessation/nicotine-addiction product markets are estimated $2.3–25 billion globally depending on methodology (too imprecise for device-specific TAM).

## Technology
Helmet-based Deep TMS delivered in-office; MDD/OCD pivotal trials used 29 treatments over 6 weeks; smoking cessation protocol used daily sessions for 3 weeks then weekly for 3 more (~18 sessions total). Requires clinical staff and specialized capital equipment, similar workflow to Neuronetics' NeuroStar.

## Novelty / Differentiation
Only device with FDA De Novo clearance for OCD (2018, first-ever non-invasive device clearance for OCD) and only TMS device cleared for smoking cessation (2020, first FDA clearance in the addiction space for any TMS device). The H-coil's broader/deeper field reaches circuitry (mPFC, ACC, insula) standard figure-8 coils cannot easily target — a genuine mechanistic differentiator, though a breadth-vs-focality tradeoff rather than unambiguous superiority.

## Limitations — Practical
Same in-clinic treatment burden as any TMS device (daily visits over weeks); requires trained staff and capital equipment; not available for at-home use; costs broadly comparable to industry-wide TMS pricing ($250–$600/session, $8,000–$12,000/course), though BrainsWay-specific self-pay pricing was not separately verified.

## Limitations — Theoretical / Readiness
Broader electric-field distribution means less spatial specificity than figure-8 coils — clinical superiority of "deeper" stimulation vs. standard TMS is still debated in head-to-head comparisons; scalp discomfort reported as somewhat higher with H-coil in some series. Smoking-cessation quit rates (19–25%), while statistically superior to sham, remain modest in absolute terms — an adjunct aid, not a standalone cure. Safety/efficacy data in special populations comparatively sparse for the newer (OCD, smoking) indications.

## Regulatory & Clinical Status
FDA clearance for MDD in 2013; De Novo clearance for OCD in 2018 (first-ever non-invasive device clearance for OCD); FDA clearance for short-term smoking cessation in adults, 2020; expanded indication for anxious depression, 2021; late-life depression labeling reported by the company (exact date not fully verified). Key registered trials: OCD pivotal NCT02229903; smoking cessation NCT02126124; long-term OCD durability follow-up NCT05404360.
