## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Mental Health / Psychiatric
**Primary Indication:** Major depressive disorder; the company has also launched a chronic-pain application in Finland and Germany ([Sooma company release](https://soomamedical.com/sooma-launches-direct-to-patient-brain-stimulation-therapy-for-depression-and-chronic-pain-in-finland-and-germany/)).

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (bilateral, via scalp electrodes)
**Delivery Mechanism:** **Transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS)** — a mild constant electrical current delivered through two electrodes in a wearable cap, increasing cortical excitability near the anode and decreasing it near the cathode ([Sooma company site](https://soomamedical.com/about-sooma/)).
**Biological basis:** Repeated tDCS sessions are proposed to induce long-term potentiation-like **neuroplastic changes** in cortical excitability that underlie its antidepressant effect — this mechanism is described in the broader tDCS-for-depression literature that Sooma cites in its own published pilot work ([Sooma-affiliated publication summary](https://soomamedical.com/publications/transcranial-direct-current-stimulation-tdcs-in-the-treatment-of-youth-depression-integrating-literature-review-insights-in-a-pilot-clinical-trial/)).

## Regulatory Status & Clinical Trial

- **EU MDR certification** achieved **September 2023** — Sooma states it was the first tDCS device manufacturer to receive certification under the EU's Medical Device Regulation ([Sooma company release](https://soomamedical.com/fda-approved-tdcs-for-at-home-depression-treatment/)).
- **FDA Investigational Device Exemption (IDE)** granted **March 11, 2025**, enabling a US pivotal trial ([Sooma company release](https://soomamedical.com/fda-ide-approval-for-sooma/)).
- US pivotal trial: **REACH-tDCS** ("Home-Based tDCS Treatment of Major Depressive Disorder"), study ID SM-001, **NCT06976697** — began June 27, 2025, recruiting as of this research pass, with primary completion estimated October 2026 ([ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06976697](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06976697)).
- **No FDA marketing clearance/approval yet** — the device remains investigational in the US; it is CE-marked/MDR-certified for EU sale.
- Sooma states it has treated **over 20,000–25,000 patients worldwide** (figures vary slightly by source date) via its EU-authorized commercial device — company-reported, not independently audited — **[unverified]**.

---

## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. Flow Neuroscience (also at-home tDCS):** Flow has FDA PMA approval (Dec 2025) for at-home depression tDCS in the US, while Sooma is still in its FDA IDE/pivotal-trial stage — a direct head-to-head regulatory race between the two leading at-home tDCS depression companies.
- **vs. Soterix Medical:** Sooma is a single-product, consumer/clinic-hybrid company focused specifically on depression (and now chronic pain), versus Soterix's broader multi-modality (tDCS+TMS+tFUS) research/clinical platform business.
- First tDCS manufacturer to receive EU MDR certification, ahead of competitors under the EU's stricter post-2021 regulatory framework.

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- **No FDA clearance/approval yet** — US regulatory outcome and timeline remain uncertain pending the REACH-tDCS pivotal trial results.
- tDCS's mechanism and effect sizes remain more contested in the peer-reviewed literature than TMS's, a field-level caveat that also applies to Sooma specifically.
- Home/self-administered use reduces clinician oversight relative to in-clinic TMS — a tradeoff shared with all at-home neuromodulation devices.
- Total funding and revenue figures beyond the disclosed €5M round were not found in this research pass — **[unverified]**.
