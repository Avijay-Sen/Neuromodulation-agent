# Soterix Medical

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Soterix Medical, Inc.
**HQ Location:** New York, New York, USA
**Founded:** 2008, to support the science of non-invasive electrical brain stimulation ([Soterix Medical — About](https://soterixmedical.com/about))
**Website:** https://soterixmedical.com

**Correction from prior draft:** the earlier version of this profile treated Soterix as a pure-play tDCS-for-depression company with an approval decision still pending. That is **materially wrong on two counts**, corrected below: (1) Soterix's actual FDA clearance for depression is for a **TMS** product, not tDCS; (2) it is a different company, **Flow Neuroscience** (also in this tracker), that received the first-ever FDA approval for a home-use tDCS depression device.

---

## Products & Technology Portfolio

Soterix Medical is a multi-modality neuromodulation company, not tDCS-only. Its portfolio includes **HD-tDCS®** (High-Definition transcranial Direct Current Stimulation, developed by Soterix in 2009), **LTE-tDCS** (Limited Total Energy tDCS), **TMS** (transcranial magnetic stimulation) products, and more recently **tFUS** (transcranial focused ultrasound) offerings, plus synergistic EEG/monitoring solutions ([Soterix Medical — research pages](https://soterixmedical.com/research/hd-tdcs); [Soterix Medical — homepage](https://soterixmedical.com/)). The company describes itself as a world leader in clinical-trial-grade non-invasive neuromodulation equipment, working with over 1,200 medical centers globally ([Soterix Medical — About](https://soterixmedical.com/about)).

## Regulatory Status

Soterix Medical has multiple distinct FDA clearances across different product lines and indications — these should not be conflated:

- **SPRY™ TMS Therapy** received **FDA clearance on March 5, 2026**, indicated for **Major Depressive Disorder** in adult patients who failed to achieve satisfactory improvement from prior antidepressant medication in the current episode ([PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/soterix-medical-announces-clearance-of-spry-transcranial-magnetic-stimulation-tms-therapy-for-the-treatment-of-major-depression-302704534.html)). **This — not tDCS — is Soterix's FDA-cleared depression product.**
- **MEGA-IOM** intraoperative neuromonitoring system: FDA 510(k) clearance, July 2024 ([Soterix Medical press release](https://soterixmedical.com/newsroom/press/2024/07/soterix-medical-announces-fda-510k-clearance-of/48)).
- **MEGA-TMS System**: FDA 510(k) clearance, August 2021 ([Soterix Medical press release](https://soterixmedical.com/newsroom/press/2021/08/soterix-medical-announces-fda-510k-clearance-for-the/40)).
- **IontoDC™ System**: FDA 510(k) clearance, May 2017 ([Soterix Medical press release](https://soterixmedical.com/research/newsroom/press/2017/05/soterix-medical-announces-fda-510k-clearance-for/28)).
- **Home-based tDCS for depression:** Soterix received FDA **Investigational Device Exemption (IDE) approval in July 2020** for a home-based tDCS "LTE" trial for depression ([Soterix Medical press release](https://soterixmedical.com/newsroom/press/2020/07/fda-grants-soterix-medical-ide-approval-for-home-based/35)) — an IDE permits a clinical trial, it is **not** a clearance/approval to market. **[unverified]** whether this program has since reached a clearance decision; no such result was found in this research pass.

**Important market context:** on **December 8, 2025**, the FDA approved a home-use tDCS device from **Flow Neuroscience** (a different company, also profiled in this tracker) for moderate-to-severe MDD — the first-ever FDA premarket approval (PMA) for a home-use non-invasive brain stimulation device specifically for depression ([Brain Stimulation journal](https://www.brainstimjrnl.com/article/S1935-861X(25)00423-1/fulltext); [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41475514/)). This is a significant competitive data point for Soterix's own home-tDCS program and should be flagged in any comparison of the two companies. *(Flow Neuroscience's own profile in this tracker should be checked/updated to confirm it reflects this approval — flagged as a follow-up, not corrected in this pass.)*

## Novelty & Differentiation

- Unlike single-modality competitors, Soterix operates across tDCS, TMS, and tFUS product lines, plus EEG/monitoring — a broader platform play than most peers in this tracker.
- Its clinical-trial-equipment business (1,200+ medical centers) is a distinct, likely larger, revenue line than any single therapeutic-device approval — relevant context for valuing the company beyond the SPRY TMS clearance alone.
