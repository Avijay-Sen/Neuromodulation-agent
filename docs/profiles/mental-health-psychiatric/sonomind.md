# SonoMind

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** SonoMind
**HQ Location:** Paris, France
**Founded:** 2024, by Jérémy Bercoff (President), Prof. Mickael Tanter, and Prof. Jean-François Aubry ([Vestbee](https://www.vestbee.com/insights/articles/sonomind-secures-3-m); [SonoMind team page](https://www.sonomind.com/notre-equipe/)). Technology is spun out of 25+ years of research at the Institute of Physics for Medicine (Inserm / ESPCI Paris–PSL / CNRS joint unit).
**Website:** https://www.sonomind.com

## Funding

- **Seed, announced Sept 24, 2025:** €3M total (~$3.53M) — €2M ($2.35M) equity seed led by Critical Path Ventures, plus €1M ($1.18M) non-dilutive grant from Bpifrance (France 2030 Deeptech program) ([PharmiWeb](https://www.pharmiweb.com/press-release/2025-09-24/sonomind-secures-3-million-to-advance-its-novel-neuromodulation-ultrasound-device-and-opens-a-new-t); [FinSMEs](https://www.finsmes.com/2025/09/sonomind-raises-e3m-in-funding.html))
- **Series A, announced ~May 12–13, 2026:** €20M (~$23.5M), led by Critical Path Ventures and Bpifrance ([MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/sonomind-23m-focused-ultrasound-depression-therapy/); [G-Medtech](https://g-medtech.com/news/sonomind-raises-e20-million-to-treat-resistant-depression-with-focused-ultrasound/)). Stated use of proceeds: fund clinical trials, targeting market authorization by 2029.

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Mental Health / Psychiatric
**Primary Indication:** Treatment-resistant depression (TRD)

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Delivery Mechanism:** Non-invasive, low-intensity (sub-thermal) focused/transcranial ultrasound, delivered via a patient-specific ("personalized") acoustic lens/metamaterial that corrects for individual skull-shape aberration, enabling precise, non-surgical targeting of deep brain structures implicated in mood regulation ([Vestbee](https://www.vestbee.com/insights/articles/sonomind-secures-3-m)).
**Biological basis:** "Deep transcranial ultrasound stimulation using personalized acoustic metamaterials improves treatment-resistant depression in humans," *Brain Stimulation*, 2025 ([full text](https://www.brainstimjrnl.com/article/S1935-861X(25)00099-3/fulltext)). General mechanism review: "Using Low-Intensity Focused Ultrasound to Treat Depression and Anxiety Disorders: A Review of Current Evidence," *PMC*, 2026 ([PMC12562471](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12562471/)) — proposed mechanisms include mechanosensitive ion-channel activation via radiation force, restoration of BDNF/dopamine/ERK/mTOR/S6K/TrkB signaling, reduced inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, IL-1β, TNF-α), increased monoamine levels, and enhanced synaptic plasticity — explicitly noted in the review as still "incompletely understood."

## Regulatory Status & Clinical Trial

**No FDA clearance, IDE, Breakthrough Device Designation, or specific NCT number for SonoMind could be verified.** All sourced coverage describes the company as pre-pivotal-trial, preparing to start clinical trials with Series A proceeds, and targeting market authorization by **2029** ([MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/sonomind-23m-focused-ultrasound-depression-therapy/)) — **[unverified]** beyond this company-stated target; no FDA designation or ClinicalTrials.gov registration was found in this research pass.

A preliminary clinical pilot at Sainte-Anne Hospital (Paris), published in *Brain Stimulation* (2025), reported a **>60% reduction in depressive-symptom severity** after 5 consecutive daily treatments, with no significant adverse events ([BioWorld](https://www.bioworld.com/articles/719927-sonominds-tus-device-reduces-depressive-symptoms); corroborated by [MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/sonomind-23m-focused-ultrasound-depression-therapy/)).

---

## Novelty & Differentiation

Per a comparative review, competing focused-ultrasound neuromodulation devices include BrainSonix (single-element, MR-compatible transducer), Attune Neuroscience (dual temporal wearable array, primarily in pain trials), and BrainBox NeuroFUS (4-element annular array). SonoMind differentiates via its patient-specific acoustic-lens/metamaterial correction for individual skull geometry, aimed at improving deep-target precision without an MRI-guided, per-session neuronavigation setup ([PMC13094473](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13094473/), "Translational prospectives for deep brain stimulation and low-intensity focused ultrasound neuromodulation," IFCN Handbook chapter, 2025/2026).

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- The mechanism of action of low-intensity focused ultrasound in mood disorders is described in independent reviews as still "incompletely understood."
- Evidence base is pilot-scale: small, open-label/early-phase, single-site data as of the funding announcements reviewed here.
- Regulatory pathway is early-stage — the company itself frames 2029 as its target market-authorization date, meaning as of this research pass it had not yet begun (or had only just begun) pivotal trials.

---

## Market Size

Treatment-resistant depression (TRD) prevalence estimates vary by definition, from 12–55% of MDD patients; a commonly cited figure is ~20–30% of MDD patients. A US claims-based study found 2.8 million of 8.9 million adults with medication-treated MDD (30.9%) met TRD criteria ([PubMed 33989464](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33989464/), "The Prevalence and National Burden of Treatment-Resistant Depression and Major Depressive Disorder in the United States"; range summary via [PMC10503923](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10503923/)).
