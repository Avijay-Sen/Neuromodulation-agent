# Neurolief

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Neurolief Ltd.
**HQ Location:** Netanya, Israel
**Founded:** Reported as 2013 by founder Amit Dar, though sources vary between 2012–2014 — **[unverified]** to the exact year ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/neurolief/__fCX-MC5jwB9f2DEU6pJOOv7k3Wwehu_8-Ns9Vd_6eS8)).
**Flagship products:** **Relivion** (acute migraine) and **Proliv Rx** (major depressive disorder)
**Website:** https://www.neurolief.com

---

## Funding

Neurolief's funding is substantially tied to strategic investment from **BrainsWay** (already covered separately in this tracker): BrainsWay closed an initial **$5 million** strategic investment (convertible loan) in Neurolief in August 2025, followed by an additional **$6 million** milestone-based convertible loan in March 2026, triggered by FDA approval of Proliv Rx ([BrainsWay investor release](https://investors.brainsway.com/news-releases/news-release-details/brainsway-completes-additional-6-million-milestone-based)). BrainsWay holds a **call option** (not a current equity stake) to acquire all outstanding Neurolief equity within defined future windows ([SEC Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1505065/000117184326001925/exh_991.htm)). Neurolief's total funding from other investors prior to the BrainsWay relationship was not independently confirmed in this research pass — **[unverified]**.

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Mental Health / Psychiatric (with an adjacent, FDA-cleared migraine/pain indication — evaluated as a borderline fit per `docs/BACKLOG.md`, included here given its newer, primary depression clearance)
**Primary Indications:** (1) Acute migraine — at-home treatment; (2) Major depressive disorder (MDD) in patients with inadequate response to antidepressants ([Neurolief PRNewswire, Proliv Rx approval](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neurolief-receives-fda-pma-approval-for-first-at-home-brain-neuromodulation-therapy-for-adults-whose-depression-was-not-adequately-improved-by-antidepressants-302658098.html)).

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Occipital and trigeminal cranial nerve branches (6 branches total), stimulated to influence brainstem and higher brain regions
**Delivery Mechanism:** A non-invasive, adjustable headset delivers modulated electrical pulses through **3 adaptive output channels** to six branches of the occipital and trigeminal nerves, with pathways conducting stimulation to the brainstem and connected regions implicated in both pain processing and mood regulation ([NeurologyLive](https://www.neurologylive.com/view/relivion-neuromodulation-system-acute-migraine-fda); [PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neurolief-receives-fda-pma-approval-for-first-at-home-brain-neuromodulation-therapy-for-adults-whose-depression-was-not-adequately-improved-by-antidepressants-302658098.html)).
**Biological basis:** Combined occipital/trigeminal nerve stimulation for migraine is grounded in the trigeminocervical complex model of migraine pathophysiology, studied in Neurolief-sponsored registered trials ([RIME Study, ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03631550](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03631550)).

## Regulatory Status & Clinical Trial

- **FDA clearance (migraine)**: Relivion cleared for at-home treatment of acute migraine in adults, based on a clinical trial of 131 migraine patients ([Medscape](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/946700); [NeurologyLive](https://www.neurologylive.com/view/relivion-neuromodulation-system-acute-migraine-fda)). That trial showed **46% of Relivion users achieved complete pain relief** vs. **11.8%** of sham-control users.
- Supporting registered trials: **RIME Study** ("Combined Occipital and Supraorbital Transcutaneous Nerve Stimulation for Treatment of Migraine"), **NCT03631550**; an earlier related study, **NCT03398668** ([ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03631550)).
- **Japan regulatory approval** for Relivion for at-home migraine treatment, announced via PRNewswire ([PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neuroliefs-relivion-secures-regulatory-approval-in-japan-pioneering-at-home-migraine-treatment-302029104.html)).
- **FDA Premarket Approval (PMA)** for **Proliv Rx** (MDD indication) granted, with approval reported around **December 2025 / announced January 12, 2026**, supported by the randomized, controlled, multicenter **MOOD Study** ([Psychiatric Times](https://www.psychiatrictimes.com/view/fda-approves-prolivrx-first-prescription-physician-directed-at-home-brain-neuromodulation-therapy-for-mdd); [PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neurolief-receives-fda-pma-approval-for-first-at-home-brain-neuromodulation-therapy-for-adults-whose-depression-was-not-adequately-improved-by-antidepressants-302658098.html)). Proliv Rx is described as the first prescription, physician-directed at-home neuromodulation therapy for MDD.

## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. NeuroSigma (Monarch eTNS):** Both target trigeminal-pathway stimulation non-invasively, but Neurolief's Relivion/Proliv Rx platform adds occipital-nerve channels and targets both migraine and depression from a shared hardware platform, a broader indication footprint than NeuroSigma's single-indication (pediatric ADHD) device.
- **vs. Flow Neuroscience/Sooma (tDCS for depression):** Proliv Rx uses multi-channel peripheral/cranial nerve stimulation rather than direct-current cortical stimulation — mechanistically distinct from the tDCS-based at-home depression competitors in this tracker.
- Dual-indication platform (migraine + depression) from one hardware base is uncommon among peer companies tracked here.

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- Financial and strategic dependence on BrainsWay via convertible loans and a call option creates **acquisition/ownership-transition risk** — Neurolief's independence could end on BrainsWay's timeline, not necessarily Neurolief's own.
- Company founding year and full independent funding history are inconsistently reported across sources — **[unverified]**.
- As with all headset-based cranial nerve stimulators, real-world adherence/compliance data for at-home, unsupervised use were not found in this research pass — **[unverified]**.
- Category placement is deliberately borderline: migraine is a pain/neurological indication, while Proliv Rx is psychiatric — investors should evaluate Neurolief against both the Mental Health/Psychiatric and Neuro-Musculoskeletal/Pain peer sets in this tracker.
