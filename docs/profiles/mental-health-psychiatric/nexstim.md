# Nexstim

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Nexstim Plc
**HQ Location:** Helsinki, Finland
**Founded:** 2000, by professor Risto Ilmoniemi and colleagues from Helsinki University of Technology's BioMag Laboratory ([Nexstim company history](https://www.nexstim.com/about-nexstim/history))
**Flagship product:** **NBT (Navigated Brain Therapy) System**
**Website:** https://www.nexstim.com

---

## Funding

Nexstim is a **publicly traded company** listed on the Helsinki Stock Exchange (Nasdaq First North Finland: **NXTMH**), with an IPO prospectus dated October 2014 ([Nexstim IPO prospectus](https://www.nexstim.com/fileadmin/Downloads/IPO/nexstim_prospectus.pdf)). Current market capitalization is approximately **€64–81 million** depending on source/date ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/equities/nexstim-ltd-he)); exact IPO proceeds and cumulative lifetime funding were not independently confirmed in this research pass — **[unverified]**.

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Mental Health / Psychiatric (also stroke motor rehabilitation and neurosurgical mapping)
**Primary Indication:** Major depressive disorder (MDD); CE-marked in Europe additionally for stroke rehabilitation and chronic neuropathic pain ([Nexstim depression page](https://www.nexstim.com/healthcare-professionals/depression-disorder)).

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Cortical regions implicated in depression (e.g., dorsolateral prefrontal cortex), individually localized per patient
**Delivery Mechanism:** **Navigated transcranial magnetic stimulation (nTMS)** — Nexstim's **SmartFocus** technology uses real-time 3D MRI-based navigation to calculate and target the induced electric field in the brain, aiming for more reproducible, patient-specific coil placement than non-navigated TMS ([Nexstim company history](https://www.nexstim.com/about-nexstim/history)).
**Biological basis:** TMS-induced cortical neuroplasticity as an antidepressant mechanism is an established, peer-reviewed area of research; navigated targeting accuracy specifically has been validated against neurosurgical motor/speech mapping, the basis of Nexstim's original FDA clearances ([Nexstim clearance history](https://www.nexstim.com/news-and-events/news/press-release/nexstim-gains-fda-clearance-to-commercialise-its-nbtr-system-in-the-us-for-the-treatment-of-depressi)).

---

## Regulatory Status & Clinical Trial

- **FDA clearance for motor mapping**: 2009; **FDA clearance for speech mapping**: 2012 — Nexstim states it remains the only company with both of these specific navigated-TMS mapping clearances ([Nexstim company history](https://www.nexstim.com/about-nexstim/history)).
- **FDA clearance for the NBT System to treat MDD**: 2018 launch in the US following clearance ([MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/nexstim-launches-nbt-major-depressive-disorder-tms-treatment-device-in-us/); [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/nexstim-gains-fda-clearance-to-commercialize-its-nbt-system-to-treat-depression)).
- **CE marked** in Europe for stroke, major depression, and chronic neuropathic pain ([Nexstim depression page](https://www.nexstim.com/healthcare-professionals/depression-disorder)).
- Phase 3 trial investigating NBT for **stroke rehabilitation** is referenced by the company; a related registered study is the electric-field-navigated 1Hz rTMS trial for post-stroke motor recovery ([ClinicalTrials.gov protocol document, NCT03010462](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/62/NCT03010462/Prot_SAP_000.pdf)).
- Real-world outcomes reported by the company: of 403 patients completing NBT treatment for MDD, **49.6% achieved remission** and **76.2% achieved clinical response** at end of treatment — company-reported registry data, not an independent RCT readout — **[unverified]** against a peer-reviewed publication.

---

## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. Neuronetics (NeuroStar) and BrainsWay:** Nexstim's core differentiator is individualized 3D electric-field navigation (SmartFocus) for coil placement, versus standardized anatomical-landmark placement used by most competing TMS systems.
- Unique dual FDA clearance history in **neurosurgical mapping** (motor 2009, speech 2012) predates and is separate from its psychiatric-indication clearance — a broader clinical footprint than TMS-only competitors.
- Multi-indication CE marking (depression + stroke + neuropathic pain) gives it a broader European label than most single-indication competitors, though the US indication remains depression-only.

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- Small public-company market capitalization (tens of millions of euros) relative to larger competitors (Neuronetics, BrainsWay), suggesting more limited commercial scale — **[unverified]** revenue figures were not independently confirmed in this pass.
- Requires in-clinic visits and specialized navigation hardware, raising cost/complexity relative to non-navigated TMS systems.
- Stroke rehabilitation indication remains investigational in the US (Phase 3) even though CE-marked in Europe — regulatory status differs by geography.
- Reported remission/response figures are company registry data rather than an independently published randomized controlled trial in the sources reviewed — **[unverified]**.
