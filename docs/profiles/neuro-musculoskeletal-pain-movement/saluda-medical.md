## Regulatory Status

Saluda Medical received **full FDA Premarket Approval (PMA, P190002) for the Evoke® Spinal Cord Stimulation System on March 8, 2022**, for chronic intractable pain — the **first time the FDA granted PMA to a spinal cord stimulation system based on results from a double-blind, randomized controlled trial** ([NeurologyLive](https://www.neurologylive.com/view/fda-approves-saluda-evoke-spinal-cord-stimulation-system-chronic-intractable-pain); [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/saludas-spinal-cord-stimulation-system-scores-fda-approval-treat-chronic-pain); [company press release](https://www.saludamedical.com/press-releases/saluda-medical-receives-fda-approval-for-the-evoke-spinal-cord-stimulation-system-to-treat-chronic-intractable/); [FDA's own device page, P190002](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/recently-approved-devices/evoke-spinal-cord-stimulation-scs-system-p190002)). Saluda separately received FDA approval for an automated programming platform for SCS ([NeuroNews International](https://neuronewsinternational.com/saluda-receives-us-fda-approval-for-automated-programming-platform-in-spinal-cord-stimulation/); [company press release](https://www.saludamedical.com/international/press-releases/saluda-medical-receives-fda-approval-for-first-automated-programming-platform-in-scs/)).

## Pivotal Clinical Trial

- **U.S. Evoke Pivotal Study** — the first double-blinded, randomized, controlled U.S. pivotal study conducted in spinal cord stimulation. 12-month results were published in **The Lancet Neurology** ([BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/saluda-medical-results-of-u-s-evoke-pivotal-study-through-12-months-published-in-the-lancet-neurology); [PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/saluda-medical-results-of-us-evoke-pivotal-study-through-12-months-published-in-the-lancet-neurology-300978602.html)).
- At **24 months**, significantly more closed-loop than open-loop patients were pain responders: **79.1% (closed-loop) vs. 53.7% (open-loop)** ([search summary of trial results]).
- A separate, earlier **Avalon Study** — a prospective, open-label, multicenter trial of 50 patients — evaluated ECAP-controlled closed-loop SCS and reported final 24-month long-term results alongside the 12-month U.S. pivotal data ([PRNewswire UK](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/saluda-medical-reports-final-long-term-results-out-to-24-months-from-avalon-study-and-results-at-12-months-from-u-s-evoke-pivotal-study-860119758.html); [MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/saluda-medical-touts-evoke-closed-loop-scs-studies/)).

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Neuro-Musculoskeletal / Pain / Movement
**Primary Indication:** Chronic intractable pain (FDA-approved indication) — includes failed back surgery syndrome and related chronic back/leg pain populations typically treated with SCS.

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Dorsal columns of the spinal cord (epidural SCS lead placement)
**Delivery Mechanism:** Implantable, closed-loop spinal cord stimulation using **ECAP (evoked compound action potential)** feedback to automatically adjust stimulation in real time — Saluda's system can deliver up to ~100 electrical pulses per second and adjusts to maintain a target neural response ([search summary]).

---

## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. open-loop SCS (Medtronic, Boston Scientific, Abbott):** Saluda's ECAP-based closed loop is the first SCS system to demonstrate PMA-level, double-blind RCT evidence of superiority in responder rate over open-loop stimulation (79.1% vs 53.7% at 24 months per the figures above) — a materially strong differentiator if that data holds up under further scrutiny.
- The March 2022 PMA (rather than the more common 510(k) pathway) reflects a higher evidentiary bar met by the pivotal trial design.

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- Standard SCS limitations apply: requires surgical implantation, lead migration/revision risk, battery replacement over device lifetime, MRI-compatibility considerations.
