# Nevro Corp. (HFX / Senza System)

**Category:** Neuro-Musculoskeletal / Pain / Movement (chronic back/leg pain, painful diabetic neuropathy) | **Status:** Acquired by Globus Medical (April 2025), formerly NYSE: NVRO | **HQ:** Redwood City, California, USA | **Founded:** 2006

## Funding & Company Status
Founded by Dr. Konstantinos Alataris. Raised ~$153M in private venture funding pre-IPO (J&J Development Corp/JJDC, NEA, Covidien, Mayo Clinic). IPO on NYSE November 2014 raised ~$126.5M. Acquired outright by Globus Medical, Inc. for $5.85/share (~$250M total equity value), announced Feb 6, 2025, completed April 3, 2025; delisted from NYSE. Globus characterized the deal as unlocking a "$2 billion market opportunity" in musculoskeletal/pain. As of Sept 2026, Nevro is not an independently traded/financed entity.

## Target Nerve / Mechanism
Epidural spinal cord stimulation (SCS) targeting the dorsal columns of the spinal cord. Percutaneous or paddle leads placed in the epidural space (typically thoracic level); an implantable pulse generator (IPG) delivers Nevro's proprietary 10 kHz ("HF10") high-frequency waveform, paresthesia-free (no tingling of older low-frequency SCS).

## Biological Basis
Classical mechanism draws on Melzack & Wall's 1965 gate control theory — stimulating large-diameter Aβ fibers in the dorsal columns is thought to inhibit C-fiber-mediated nociceptive transmission at the dorsal horn ("closing the gate") [Jensen et al., *European Journal of Pain*, 2019]. More recent mechanistic reviews extend this to neuroimmune/glial modulation and descending serotonergic/opioid inhibition [PMC8331196, 2021; PMC13512981, 2025]. Pivotal clinical evidence: SENZA-RCT, Kapural et al., *Anesthesiology*, 2015;123(4):851-860, with 12-month follow-up in *Neurosurgery*.

## Market Size / Affected Population
~20.4% of US adults (~50 million) live with chronic pain, 8% (~19.6 million) have "high-impact" chronic pain (CDC-sourced). Lifetime prevalence of low back pain is 60–70% in industrialized countries [PMC4603263]; annual US economic burden of low back pain ~$100–200 billion. ~34,000 SCS devices implanted worldwide annually, about half in North America. Global SCS device market: ~$2.1B (2022) → $4.8B (2032) per Allied Market Research; ~$3.5–3.8B (2025/2026) → $6.5–7.5B (2032–2035) per Fortune Business Insights. Globus Medical pegged the addressable opportunity Nevro unlocks at ~$2 billion.

## Technology
(1) Trial period tests efficacy with temporary percutaneous leads; (2) if successful, leads permanently implanted in epidural space over dorsal columns; (3) leads connect to a rechargeable IPG implanted subcutaneously; (4) IPG delivers continuous 10 kHz stimulation, sub-perception (no paresthesia); (5) newer HFX iQ platform (FDA-approved Oct 12, 2022) adds "HFX AdaptivAI" — algorithmic personalization adjusting therapy based on patient-reported outcomes and usage data, marketed as the first AI-driven SCS system.

## Novelty / Differentiation
Core differentiation vs. Medtronic, Boston Scientific, and Abbott is the paresthesia-free 10 kHz waveform, validated head-to-head in the SENZA-RCT. Only SCS system with an FDA label specifically for painful diabetic neuropathy (approved July 19, 2021) and non-surgical refractory back pain (expanded label, 2022). The AI-personalization angle (HFX iQ/AdaptivAI) is a further differentiator vs. competitors' manual programming.

## Limitations — Practical
Requires invasive surgical implantation and a preceding percutaneous trial period; lead migration well-documented (13.2% in a review of 68 studies/2,700+ patients; some literature reports 20–22.6%) [PMC9018457]; surgical-site infection requiring reoperation ~2.9% of cases [PubMed 37542526]; overall complication/revision rates range ~5.3–40% depending on definition/follow-up. MRI compatibility is conditional. Ongoing programming and periodic battery recharging required.

## Limitations — Theoretical / Readiness
Durability of pain relief over many years is debated — Nevro cites 60–80% "long-term responder" rates at 12 months, but independent longer-horizon data are more limited. The evidence base for SCS in chronic low back pain specifically has been directly disputed in the literature: a 2023 paper rebuts a Cochrane Library review's negative conclusions on SCS efficacy for low back pain [PMC10452059]. Mechanistic understanding of why 10 kHz outperforms lower frequencies remains incompletely characterized. Reimbursement is well-established for SCS broadly — Nevro's most de-risked dimension.

## Regulatory & Clinical Status
Senza System (10 kHz) — original FDA PMA approval, May 2015. Expanded indications: painful diabetic neuropathy (PDN), approved July 19, 2021; non-surgical refractory back pain (NSRBP), expanded label, 2022. HFX iQ system (AI-personalized SCS), FDA-approved October 12, 2022, CE Marked in Europe (2024). Commercially available in US, Europe, Australia. As of April 2025, acquired by Globus Medical and delisted from NYSE.
