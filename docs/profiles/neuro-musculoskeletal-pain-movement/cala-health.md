# Cala Health

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Cala Health, Inc.
**HQ Location:** San Francisco Bay Area, California, USA (Stanford Biodesign spinout)
**Founded:** 2013–2014 ([Stanford Biodesign](https://med.stanford.edu/biodesign/our-impact/technologies/cala-health.html))
**Website:** https://calahealth.com

**Major correction from prior draft:** the earlier version of this profile described the Cala Trio as still investigational, awaiting a De Novo FDA decision. **This is wrong — the device has been FDA cleared since April 2018.** Corrected below.

---

## Regulatory Status

- **Cala Trio™** received **FDA De Novo clearance in April 2018** for the **transient relief of hand tremor in adults with essential tremor** ([Essential Tremor Foundation — company press release copy, April 26, 2018](https://www.essentialtremor.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/CalaHealth-FDAClearance-2018Apr26.pdf)). It is described as the first non-invasive, prescription, targeted therapy for essential tremor ([Pacific Neuroscience Institute fact sheet](https://www.pacificneuroscienceinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Cala-Trio-Fact-Sheet-2020.pdf)).
- A second product, **Cala kIQ™**, received FDA clearance in **2023** as a wearable **Transcutaneous Afferent Patterned Stimulation (TAPS)** system for hand tremor in essential tremor **and Parkinson's disease** ([search summary — verify exact clearance date directly against FDA's 510(k)/De Novo database before citing]).
- Development timeline: proof-of-concept studies began 2014; a multicenter randomized controlled trial completed 2016; FDA clearance followed in 2018 ([Stanford Biodesign](https://med.stanford.edu/biodesign/our-impact/technologies/cala-health.html)).

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Neuro-Musculoskeletal / Pain / Movement
**Primary Indication:** Essential tremor (hand tremor) — FDA-cleared
**Secondary Indication:** Parkinson's disease tremor — via Cala kIQ, **[unverified]** exact scope/label language; check FDA database directly.

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Median and radial nerves at the wrist
**Delivery Mechanism:** Non-invasive wearable stimulator (worn on wrist) delivering Transcutaneous Afferent Patterned Stimulation (TAPS) — patterned electrical stimulation of peripheral nerves, on-demand ([Medical Design & Outsourcing](https://www.medicaldesignandoutsourcing.com/cala-health-trio-device-treats-essential-tremors-kate-rosenbluth/)).

---

## Novelty & Differentiation

- Cala Trio is described in company/industry sources as **the only non-invasive prescription therapy for essential tremor** ([search summary]) — a genuinely differentiated market position versus invasive options (DBS, MRgFUS thalamotomy) and versus pharmacotherapy.
- Backed by "largest-ever clinical study of essential tremor patients" per company claims ([search summary — verify trial size/name directly before citing a specific number]).

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- **[unverified]** — specific tremor-reduction efficacy percentages, adherence rates, and long-term (multi-year) durability data were not independently confirmed against a primary clinical-trial source in this research pass; the pivotal trial completed in 2016 per Stanford Biodesign's timeline — pull the actual trial publication (likely in *Tremor and Other Hyperkinetic Movements* or a neurology journal) before citing specific efficacy numbers.
- Standard limitations of wearable, on-demand peripheral stimulation apply: requires daily/session-based use, electrode/skin-contact considerations, and is not curative (effect is transient per the FDA-cleared label language: "transient relief").
