# SetPoint Medical Corporation

**Category:** Neuro-Musculoskeletal / Pain / Movement (rheumatoid arthritis / autoimmune inflammatory disease) | **Status:** Private | **HQ:** Valencia, California, USA | **Founded:** 2007

## Funding & Company Status
Built on Dr. Kevin Tracey's discovery of the "inflammatory reflex." Funding-tracker aggregates report cumulative totals ~$388–389M over 12–13 rounds (approximate). Most recently verified: $140M private financing announced August 11, 2025 ($25M second tranche of Series C + $115M Series D, co-led by Elevage Medical Technologies and Ally Bridge Group), to fund commercialization following FDA approval.

## Target Nerve / Mechanism
Cervical vagus nerve stimulation via a small implanted neurostimulator placed directly on the left vagus nerve in the neck. Delivers brief (~1 minute/day), low-intensity electrical pulses intended to engage the cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway and suppress systemic pro-inflammatory cytokine production.

## Biological Basis
Built on the "inflammatory reflex"/cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway (CAP): efferent vagal signaling releases acetylcholine binding α7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptors (α7nAChR) on macrophages, suppressing TNF, IL-1β, HMGB1, and other cytokines. Foundational: Borovikova LV, et al., *Nature*, 2000; Tracey KJ, "The inflammatory reflex," *Nature*, 2002. Modern review: PubMed 35300992, 2022. Direct clinical validation for RA: RESET-RA pivotal trial, *Nature Medicine* (12-month outcomes); initial results PMC10935935.

## Market Size / Affected Population
RA affects ~0.2–1% of adults globally [*Nature Reviews Rheumatology*, 2022]. RA is ~20% of ~52 million US arthritis cases (rough order-of-magnitude, treat cautiously). Global pooled prevalence of "difficult-to-treat RA" (D2T-RA) at 11.7% of RA patients, stricter definitions (≥3 or ≥4 failed lines) at 4.3% and 1.6% [PubMed 41188120, 2025] — the population SetPoint's label most directly targets. Bioelectronic medicine device category variously sized at $23–30B (2024/2025) growing to $33–54B by 2030 (wide variance by category definition); no RA-vagus-stimulation-specific TAM figure found.

## Technology
(1) Cuff-style micro-stimulator ("SetPoint Implant") surgically placed directly on the cervical vagus nerve; (2) powered by rechargeable lithium-ion battery (Zero Volt™ technology) with wireless RF recharging; (3) automatically delivers ~1 minute of stimulation per day, designed to last up to 10 years without replacement; (4) intended to chronically engage the inflammatory reflex, reducing systemic inflammatory cytokine tone and RA disease activity (ACR20/ACR50, DAS28 scores).

## Novelty / Differentiation
Distinct mechanism class from every other company in this cohort — targets systemic immune-inflammatory biology rather than pain/motor circuits, positioning against pharmacologic RA therapies (TNF/IL-6/JAK inhibitors) rather than other neurostim companies. Device-based, once-daily one-minute regimen with no chronic systemic drug exposure, targeting patients who already failed biologics. First FDA-approved neuroimmune-modulation implant for any indication.

## Limitations — Practical
Requires surgical implantation directly on the vagus nerve; RESET-RA reported perioperative serious adverse events in ~1.6% of patients (all resolved); requires regular wireless recharging; external charger/docking station explicitly MR Unsafe (implant itself MR-conditional at 1.5T/3.0T two weeks post-implant). Near-term US availability limited — rollout to select US cities first, full nationwide availability targeted early 2026.

## Limitations — Theoretical / Readiness
The cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway has two-plus decades of preclinical support and now a positive pivotal human RCT, but remains young relative to biologic/DMARD pharmacology (25+ years of real-world data, millions of patients). RESET-RA effect sizes (ACR20 35.2% active vs 24.2% sham at 3 months, P=0.0209) are statistically significant but modest vs. some biologic response rates; durability beyond ~12-month open-label follow-up not yet established. Reimbursement pathways for a brand-new device category are nascent.

## Regulatory & Clinical Status
FDA Premarket Approval (PMA, P240039) granted July 31, 2025, for adults with moderately-to-severely active RA with inadequate response/intolerance to ≥1 biologic or targeted synthetic DMARD. Pivotal trial: RESET-RA (NCT04539964) — multicenter, randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled, up to 250 patients across 40 US sites, enrolled 242; primary efficacy endpoint (ACR20) met — 35.2% (active) vs. 24.2% (sham) at 3 months (P=0.0209), improving to 50.0% at 6 months and 52.8% at 12 months open-label follow-up; published in *Nature Medicine*.
