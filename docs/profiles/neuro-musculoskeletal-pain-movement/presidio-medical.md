## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Neuro-Musculoskeletal / Pain / Movement
**Primary Indication:** Chronic nociceptive (axial) low back pain ([Medical Economics](https://www.medicaleconomics.com/view/presidio-medical-wins-fda-ide-approval-for-neuromodulation-trial-names-new-cfo)).

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Spinal cord (epidural space, dorsal column region)
**Delivery Mechanism:** **Ultra-low frequency (ULF) spinal cord stimulation** — delivers epidural currents at frequencies far below conventional SCS, aiming to produce a **reversible conduction block** of pain-signaling neurons via **sodium channel inactivation**, rather than the paresthesia-masking or gate-control mechanisms used by conventional/high-frequency SCS ([MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/presidio-fda-ide-for-ulf-neuromod/); [company release](https://presidiomedical.com/press-releases/presidio-medicaltm-receives-ide-approval-for-ultra-low-frequency-ulftm-neuromodulation-clinical-study-and-appoints-dimas-jimenez-as-chief-financial-officer/)).
**Biological basis:** Presidio-sponsored research published in *Science* (2021) demonstrated that ULF waveforms produce a rapidly developing, completely reversible conduction block in animal models, and reported a 2-week human trial testing epidural ULF currents in 20 subjects with chronic leg and back pain ([Presidio company release summarizing the Science publication](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210825005742/en/Presidio-Medical-Announces-Publication-of-ULF-Neuromodulation-Research-in-Science-Translational-Medicine)) — note the company release title references *Science Translational Medicine* while search summaries reference *Science*; the exact journal should be confirmed against the original citation before further use — **[unverified]** journal name discrepancy.

## Regulatory Status & Clinical Trial

- **FDA Investigational Device Exemption (IDE)** approved for the **FULFILL Study** — a prospective, multi-center, randomized, controlled pivotal trial comparing ULF spinal cord stimulation plus conservative medical management (CMM) to CMM alone for chronic axial low back pain with a prominent nociceptive etiology, to be run in the US and Australia (**NCT06763653**) ([ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06763653); [MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/presidio-fda-ide-for-ulf-neuromod/)).
- An earlier first-in-human feasibility trial (**NCT05837234**) reported that **53% of patients** who received ULF SCS experienced **80–100% pain relief** — early feasibility data, not yet confirmatory ([search-summarized reporting](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05837234)).
- No FDA marketing approval yet — device remains investigational, pending the FULFILL pivotal trial.

---

## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. Nevro (10kHz paresthesia-free SCS) and Saluda (closed-loop ECAP-based SCS):** Presidio's ULF approach is mechanistically distinct — it targets a **reversible nerve conduction block via sodium channel inactivation** rather than paresthesia masking or closed-loop amplitude titration, positioning it as a third, mechanistically novel branch of implanted SCS technology in this tracker.
- Backed by a peer-reviewed mechanistic publication (2021) preceding its pivotal trial, providing stronger mechanistic grounding at an earlier stage than is typical for early-stage neurostimulation companies.
