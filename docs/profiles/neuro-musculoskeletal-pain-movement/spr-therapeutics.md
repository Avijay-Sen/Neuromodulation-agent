# SPR Therapeutics

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** SPR Therapeutics, Inc.
**HQ Location:** Cleveland (Highland Hills), OH, USA
**Founded:** 2010, by Maria Bennett, growing out of her biomedical-engineering graduate research at **Case Western Reserve University** ([Crain's Cleveland Business](https://www.crainscleveland.com/real-estate/medical-device-company-spr-therapeutics-expands-headquarters-highland-hills); [Greater Cleveland Partnership](https://greatercle.com/blog/gcp-news/cleveland-continues-to-revolutionize-medical-research-at-spr-therapeutics/))
**Flagship product:** **SPRINT PNS System** (endura single-lead / extensa dual-lead)
**Website:** https://www.sprpainrelief.com

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Neuro-Musculoskeletal / Pain / Movement
**Primary Indication:** Chronic and acute pain in the back and/or extremities, with an expanded indication covering areas of the head, neck, and front of the torso ([Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/orthopedics/20211105/fda-clears-spr-therapeutics-peripheral-nerve-stimulation-system-for-head-neck-torso)).

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Peripheral nerves near the site of pain (percutaneous lead placement)
**Delivery Mechanism:** **Percutaneous peripheral nerve stimulation (PNS)** — a thin lead is placed near a target peripheral nerve for up to 60 days, delivering stimulation via an external wearable pulse generator, then removed (non-permanent implant), distinguishing it from fully implanted PNS/SCS systems ([SPR Indications page](https://www.sprpainrelief.com/indications); [PharmaSalmanac](https://www.pharmasalmanac.com/articles/spr-therapeutics-single-and-dual-lead-peripheral-nerve-stimulation-devices-pass-fda)).
**Biological basis:** As with other peripheral nerve stimulators in this tracker, SPRINT's proposed mechanism rests on the **gate control theory of pain** (Melzack & Wall, 1965), in which stimulation of large-diameter afferent fibers inhibits nociceptive transmission at the dorsal horn ([Mechanism of Action of Peripheral Nerve Stimulation for Chronic Pain — PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10003676/)); SPR's own trial registrations reference sustained pain relief outlasting the 60-day treatment period, suggesting an additional neuroplastic/central-modulation component beyond simple gating — **[unverified]** mechanistic detail beyond the general PNS literature.

## Regulatory Status & Clinical Trial

- **Original FDA clearance for the SPRINT PNS System**: **2016** ([search-summarized industry reporting](https://www.mddionline.com/business/spr-therapeutics-wins-nod-for-dual-lead-pns-system)) — **[unverified]** against a primary FDA 510(k) database record in this pass.
- **FDA clearance for the "endura" and "extensa" (single- and dual-lead) SPRINT systems**, confirming SPRINT as the only percutaneous PNS system cleared for up to 60 days of use in the back and/or extremities for both chronic and acute pain ([SPR company release](https://www.sprpainrelief.com/press-releases/fda-clears-sprs-non-opioid-sprint-endura-and-extensa-peripheral-nerve-stimulation)).
- **Expanded FDA clearance (October 2021)** extending indications to areas of the head, neck, and front of torso ([Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/orthopedics/20211105/fda-clears-spr-therapeutics-peripheral-nerve-stimulation-system-for-head-neck-torso); [Medical Alley](https://www.medicalalley.org/fda-clearance-of-an-expanded-indication-for-the-sprint-pns-system-now-enables-treatment-in-areas-of-the-head-neck-and-torso/)).
- Registered trials include the **RESET** trial for back pain (**NCT04246281**) and a post-operative orthopedic-pain trial (**NCT04341948**) ([ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04246281)).

## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. Nalu Medical/SetPoint Medical (fully implanted PNS):** SPRINT is deliberately **non-permanent** — a percutaneous lead used for up to 60 days, then removed, avoiding the surgical-implant risk profile of permanent IPG-based systems, at the cost of requiring reapplication if pain recurs.
- Only percutaneous PNS system FDA-cleared for **both chronic and acute pain**, a broader acute-pain indication than most competing implanted systems, which typically target chronic pain only.
- Backed by meaningful non-dilutive DoD/NIH funding (unusual for this tracker's typical VC-only-funded companies), reflecting interest from military pain-management use cases (e.g., post-amputation, combat injury).

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- Being **temporary/percutaneous** rather than permanently implanted means patients may require repeat procedures if pain recurs after lead removal — a different treatment-burden profile than permanent implants.
- Exact original 2016 FDA clearance details were not independently confirmed against a primary FDA source in this pass — **[unverified]**.
- Total lifetime equity funding beyond the disclosed $37M Series D was not found — **[unverified]**.
- General field-level limitation shared with all gate-control-theory-based PNS devices: individual patient response variability and lack of a universally accepted predictive biomarker for responders.
