# Nēsos (auricular vagus nerve stimulator for rheumatoid arthritis)

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Nēsos Corp (device previously referred to as "Vorso" / PROTECT System)
**HQ Location:** Redwood City, CA, USA
**Founded:** 2017, co-founded by Konstantinos Alataris, who previously founded and led Nevro (maker of the Senza spinal cord stimulator) ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/nesos); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90584087/nesos-brain-stimulating-ear-buds-rheumatoid-arthritis))

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Neuro-Musculoskeletal / Pain / Movement (bioelectronic/neuroimmune)
**Primary Indication:** Rheumatoid arthritis (RA), particularly patients with an inadequate response to or intolerance of DMARDs.

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Auricular branch of the vagus nerve (innervating cutaneous regions of the external ear)
**Delivery Mechanism:** A non-invasive, in-ear "earbud"-like wearable device delivers electrical stimulation pulses to the auricular vagus nerve, worn approximately 15 minutes daily, aiming to engage the brain's "inflammatory reflex" and modulate systemic immune/cytokine activity — a concept the company calls "e-mmunotherapy" ([Technology Networks](https://www.technologynetworks.com/neuroscience/blog/interview-behind-the-e-mmunotherapy-earbud-that-could-treat-arthritis-349324); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90584087/nesos-brain-stimulating-ear-buds-rheumatoid-arthritis)).
**Biological basis:** The foundational mechanistic study is Koopman et al., "Vagus nerve stimulation inhibits cytokine production and attenuates disease severity in rheumatoid arthritis," *PNAS*, 2016, showing vagal efferent activity reduces TNF via the cholinergic anti-inflammatory pathway (α7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptor-mediated) ([PNAS abstract](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2016PNAS..113.8284K/abstract)). Direct clinical VNS-for-RA evidence appears in Genovese et al., "Safety and efficacy of neurostimulation with a miniaturised vagus nerve stimulation device in patients with multidrug-refractory rheumatoid arthritis," *The Lancet Rheumatology*, 2020 ([The Lancet Rheumatology](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanrhe/article/PIIS2665-9913(20)30172-7/abstract)) — **note:** this pivotal study used an *implantable* "MicroRegulator" device developed by SetPoint Medical, not Nēsos's non-invasive auricular device; it is cited here as the mechanism-underpinning literature for cholinergic anti-inflammatory VNS broadly, not as a Nēsos-sponsored trial.

## Regulatory Status & Clinical Trial

- **FDA Breakthrough Device Designation** announced **April 22, 2021**, for the digital therapeutic targeting RA patients with inadequate response to or intolerance of DMARDs (secondary-source reporting; primary pages could not be directly fetched in this research pass — **[unverified]** against a primary FDA source).
- **No FDA clearance or approval** for the Nēsos device was found in this research pass.
- Registered trial: **NCT04306744**, "The RA PROTECTION Study" (official title references the "Vorso PROTECT System"), sponsored by Nēsos Corp — a prospective, multicenter, double-blind, randomized, controlled pilot trial in biologic/tsDMARD-naïve active RA patients, device used once daily for 12 weeks alongside stable methotrexate; study start ~Sept 2020, primary completion ~Jan 2022 ([ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04306744](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04306744) — details as reported in secondary search summaries; direct primary-record confirmation recommended).
- A published proof-of-concept report describes an average DAS28-CRP reduction of 1.4 with no serious adverse events ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2665991320304252)).

## Novelty & Differentiation

Nēsos's key differentiator versus SetPoint Medical (whose implantable cervical VNS device reportedly achieved FDA approval as the first non-drug treatment for RA, backed by the 242-patient RESET-RA pivotal trial) is being **fully non-invasive** — an in-ear device requiring no implant or surgery, versus SetPoint's surgically implanted neck device. This represents a materially different risk, reimbursement, and adoption profile ([MedCentral](https://www.medcentral.com/rheumatology/rheumatoid-arthritis/fda-approves-first-ever-vagus-nerve-stimulator-for-rheumatoid-arthritis); [SetPoint Medical FAQs](https://setpointmedical.com/faqs/)).

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- Non-invasive auricular VNS delivers a weaker, less targeted stimulus than a surgically implanted cervical electrode, raising open questions about dose consistency and durability of immunomodulatory effect relative to SetPoint's implant, which already has larger (242-patient) pivotal data and a reported FDA approval.
- Nēsos's own published human data is limited to a small pilot/proof-of-concept study, with no Phase 3 or pivotal results found in this research pass.
- No funding disclosed since 2020, and no product launch or additional regulatory milestones found since the 2021 Breakthrough Device announcement — **[unverified]** current operating status.

## Market Size

Rheumatoid arthritis affects roughly **10.6 million diagnosed cases** among US adults with diagnosed arthritis (15.8% of ~58.5 million US adults with diagnosed arthritis), per CDC data (2017–March 2020 data, published 2025); women are affected roughly 3x more often than men ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/pcd/issues/2025/24_0393.htm)).
