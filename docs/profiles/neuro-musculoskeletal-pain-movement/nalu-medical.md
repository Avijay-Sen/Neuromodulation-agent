# Nalu Medical

## Company Profile

**Company Name:** Nalu Medical, Inc.
**HQ Location:** Carlsbad, CA, USA
**Founded:** 2014 ([Craft.co company profile](https://craft.co/nalu-medical))
**Flagship product:** **Nalu Neurostimulation System** (micro-IPG)
**Status:** **Acquired by Boston Scientific** — deal completed January 27, 2026
**Website:** https://nalumed.com

---

## Funding & Acquisition

Nalu Medical raised **$65 million** in a growth financing round to accelerate commercial expansion ([MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/nalu-medical-raises-65m-neurostim-tech/)). Boston Scientific, which had been an investor in Nalu since 2017 holding an approximate **9% equity stake**, announced an agreement in October 2025 to acquire the remainder of the company; the acquisition **closed January 27, 2026** for an upfront cash payment of approximately **$523 million, net of cash acquired** (total transaction value reported at **~$533 million**) ([Boston Scientific press release](https://news.bostonscientific.com/2025-10-17-Boston-Scientific-Announces-Agreement-to-Acquire-Nalu-Medical,-Inc); [BioXconomy](https://www.bioxconomy.com/partnering/boston-scientific-expands-pain-management-portfolio-with-533m-nalu-medical-buy)). The Nalu business has been integrated into **Boston Scientific's Neuromodulation division** ([MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/boston-scientific-completes-nalu-medical-acquisition/)).

## Clinical Indication

**Disorder Category:** Neuro-Musculoskeletal / Pain / Movement
**Primary Indication:** Chronic, intractable pain of peripheral nerve origin (e.g., shoulder, lower back, knee) and spinal cord stimulation (SCS) indications ([Boston Scientific press release](https://news.bostonscientific.com/2025-10-17-Boston-Scientific-Announces-Agreement-to-Acquire-Nalu-Medical,-Inc)).

---

## Target Nerve or Mechanism

**Anatomical Target:** Peripheral nerves (PNS) and spinal cord (SCS), per indication
**Delivery Mechanism:** The **Nalu micro-IPG** is a miniaturized, battery-free implantable pulse generator wirelessly powered by an externally worn Therapy Disc and controlled via a smartphone app; mild electrical impulses interrupt pain signals before they reach the brain ([ExpertPainCare](https://www.expertpaincare.com/blog/how-the-novel-nalu-micro-ipg-pns-system-redefines-the-chronic-pain-treatment-paradigm); [Boston Scientific press release](https://news.bostonscientific.com/2025-10-17-Boston-Scientific-Announces-Agreement-to-Acquire-Nalu-Medical,-Inc)).
**Biological basis:** Peripheral nerve stimulation for chronic pain is grounded in the **gate control theory of pain** (Melzack & Wall, 1965) — stimulating large-diameter, non-nociceptive fibers activates inhibitory interneurons that suppress nociceptive signal transmission in the dorsal horn; see the modern mechanistic review ([Mechanism of Action of Peripheral Nerve Stimulation for Chronic Pain: A Narrative Review — PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10003676/)) and the golden-anniversary retrospective on the original theory ([Gate control theory 50th anniversary — PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4676495/)).

## Novelty & Differentiation

- **vs. SetPoint Medical/Saluda/Neuros Medical (implanted neurostimulation peers):** Nalu's micro-IPG is fully battery-free and wirelessly powered, with an expected **18-year service life** claimed by the company, differentiating it from battery-based competing IPGs that require periodic replacement.
- Dual-indication clearance for both PNS and SCS from one implant platform is a differentiator relative to single-indication peers.
- Acquisition by Boston Scientific (a top-3 global neuromodulation incumbent) at a ~$533M valuation validates the platform commercially, but also removes it from the independent-startup landscape investors would otherwise be evaluating.

---

## Limitations: Practical & Theoretical

- **No longer an independent company** — as of January 2026 it is wholly owned by Boston Scientific; VC-style investment access to Nalu specifically no longer exists.
- Exact FDA clearance date (2020) and specific clinical trial outcome data were not independently verified against primary FDA/journal sources in this pass — **[unverified]**.
- General field-level limitation shared with all peripheral/spinal implants: infection risk, lead migration, and eventual device revision surgery.
